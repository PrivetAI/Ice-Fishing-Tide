# Ice Fishing Tide

## Subtitle
Water Level & Moon Phase Fishing Predictor

## Description
Ice Fishing Tide helps ice fishermen predict the best times to fish based on water level changes, tides, and moon phases. The app simulates water level fluctuations throughout the day based on your water body type and lunar cycles.

Features:
- Water body selection (River, Reservoir, Lake with Inflow)
- Interactive water level chart showing optimal fishing zones
- Moon phase calendar with fishing quality indicators  
- Personal fishing log to track your catches
- Day comparison to find patterns
- Educational content about tides and fish behavior

Perfect for planning your next ice fishing trip!
